Project Overview

Project Name

Hedonics Visibility Boost

Project Overview

  • Purpose: Build an awareness site to showcase Hedonics Video Marketing’s portfolio and services, improve search visibility, and drive new leads.
  • Intended Users: Small-to-medium business owners, marketing managers, and independent content creators seeking professional video marketing services.
  • Content Overview:
    • Home: Hero banner + brief intro
    • About: Company story & team
    • Services: Offered packages & features
    • Portfolio: Embedded video gallery
    • Contact: Lead-capture form

Wireframe

Site Map

  1. Home — introduction, hero banner, CTAs
  2. About — company story, mission, team
  3. Services — video packages, features
  4. Portfolio — embedded video reels
  5. Contact — form + contact info

Page Designs

Home

  • Purpose: Greet visitors, highlight value prop
  • Audience: Prospective clients
  • Content: Hero image/video, tagline, buttons to Services/Contact
  • Interactivity: Smooth scroll on button click

About

  • Purpose: Build credibility via story & team bios
  • Audience: Decision-makers evaluating agencies
  • Content: Text, team photos
  • Interactivity: Hover-reveal bios

Services

  • Purpose: Detail packages & pricing
  • Audience: Budget-conscious shoppers
  • Content: Cards with service names, descriptions, “Learn More” links
  • Interactivity: Expand/collapse details

Portfolio

  • Purpose: Showcase past work
  • Audience: Visual learners
  • Content: Embedded video gallery
  • Interactivity: Lightbox playback

Contact

  • Purpose: Capture leads
  • Audience: Ready-to-convert prospects
  • Content: Form (name, email, message), map embed
  • Validation: Required fields via JavaScript

Dynamic Functionality

  • Video Lightbox: Opens portfolio videos in overlay (jQuery Lightbox plugin).
  • Form Validation: JavaScript checks required fields before submit.
  • Smooth Scrolling: On-page anchors animate scrolling.
  • AJAX Contact: Submits Contact form without full page reload using vanilla JS (example: https://www.w3schools.com/howto/howto_js_ajax_contact.asp).
